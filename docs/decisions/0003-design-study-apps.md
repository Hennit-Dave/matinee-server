# 0003 — The 5 apps to study

Date: 2026-11-10 · Status: accepted · Decided by: Dave

## Problem
The brief requires studying 5 well-made apps, with screenshots and one line each on why it works, in the docs.

## Decision
Peacock, Hulu, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+, HBO Max.

## Reason
All five are polished, content-first movie apps: browsing, posters, detail pages, dark UI, skeletons, motion.

## Consequences
- All five are streaming apps. None has a seat map, a timed hold, or a ticket checkout, so those flows have no reference in this set.
- Write-up goes in `docs/DESIGN-STUDY.md`: screenshot + one line per app.
