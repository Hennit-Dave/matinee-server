## 7. Read-your-own-writes, with a replica that never hears about the booking (1.5 s window here)
Alice booked the seat. The "replica" has no booking row; the primary has 1.
  an anonymous reader sees   available   (stale replica)
  Bob (has an id) sees       available   (stale replica)
  Alice (just booked) sees   sold   (read from the primary)
  Bob taps it anyway: HTTP 409 "seat_taken"   (the authoritative check is on the primary)
  after the window, Alice sees available   (back on the replica)
