READ THIS AS:
  The load test sends 500 users from ONE address at the SAME 50 seats. The per-ADDRESS rate limits (hold 40/min, booking 20/min)
  are right in production — they are the backstop against one address hoarding — and they refuse this test before the hold logic
  runs, so the run cannot prove the no-double-booking guarantee. 460 hold attempts and 7 of the 27 hold-winners' bookings are
  refused with 429, and only 20 bookings land. That is exactly the gap ADR 0016 handed to 5e ("the load test sends 500 users from
  one address, which the address limits will refuse; 5e needs a way to lift them"): the profile must lift the per-ADDRESS limits
  while keeping the per-install limits (each of the 500 users is its own install doing one honest hold + booking).
